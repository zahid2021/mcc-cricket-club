Our Sponsor

MUSTAFATHE HEART BEHIND THE BADGE

Mustafa is the proud sponsor of Mustafa Cricket Club. From Australia, he supports the club remotely — funding the game so our boys can focus on cricket, discipline, and dreams.

Remote from Australia

Based in Australia, Mustafa stays connected and keeps the club moving with steady guidance and support.

Tournament Fees

He covers tournament entry fees so children can compete without financial pressure on their families.

Kit & Equipment

Uniforms, bats, balls, and match essentials — Mustafa provides the gear our squad needs to play properly.

Full Club Expenses

He bears the total running cost of the club — giving every boy a fair chance to wear the badge with pride.

ONE SPONSOR. EVERY EXPENSE COVERED.

From practice days to tournament nights, Mustafa funds the club so talent is never limited by money. Kits, fees, travel support, and day-to-day cricket costs — he carries the full responsibility so the boys can simply play.
